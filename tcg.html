A cool fact about myself is that I am a professional in the TCG sphere, competing in many regionals, and even making it to the world championships in various games!

Trading card games provide an experience that not many other games can.

I personally enjoy these things when it comes to trading card games.

  1. I love how they bring you and another face to face interacting with one another while still enjoying a common game.
  2. The cards art and gameplay of each game can feel so variably different and interesting, almost as if there is one for everyone.
  3. The actual games you play with other people will almost always be different, due to the natural variance of cards.

The games that I personally play are Grand Archive, Magic, and Cardfight Vanguard.

While these games may be on the more niche side, they are incredibly fun!

I love these games to death, and in fact, have even made content on them!
